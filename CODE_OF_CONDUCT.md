# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CyberPhotonics-SPR is intended to be a respectful, technically rigorous, and research-integrity-focused community. Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, users, and collaborators are expected to help create an environment where people can participate without harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or hostility.

We value disagreement about engineering and scientific methods when it is expressed constructively and supported by evidence.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- communicating respectfully and professionally;
- giving and receiving technical criticism in good faith;
- distinguishing facts, measurements, assumptions, simulations, and opinions;
- acknowledging limitations and uncertainty;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and responsible disclosure;
- focusing review comments on code, evidence, methods, and documented behavior rather than individuals;
- helping new contributors understand project standards;
- correcting mistakes transparently when new evidence becomes available.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or targeted intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate public disclosure of another person's private information;
- publishing vulnerability details contrary to `SECURITY.md`;
- knowingly falsifying scientific results, experimental provenance, benchmark identity, or evidence classification;
- pressuring contributors to present synthetic or surrogate results as physical measurements;
- sabotaging project infrastructure, reviews, release evidence, or collaboration spaces;
- sustained personal attacks or disruption unrelated to constructive technical discussion.

## Research integrity

Because this repository supports publication-oriented research, deliberate manipulation of evidence is treated as a serious conduct issue. Examples include fabricating experimental measurements, altering provenance to conceal artifact identity, or intentionally misrepresenting numerical or device results.

Good-faith mistakes, model disagreement, failed experiments, negative results, and reproducible scientific debate are not misconduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, releases, project documentation, official project communication channels, and other spaces where someone is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting conduct concerns

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact a project maintainer through an appropriate GitHub channel. If a report contains a security vulnerability, sensitive personal information, credentials, or exploit details, follow `SECURITY.md` and do not publish those details publicly.

Reports should include enough context to understand what happened, where it occurred, and what response is requested. Maintainers should handle reports with discretion and avoid unnecessary disclosure of reporter or subject information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take proportionate action including clarification, warning, moderation of project spaces, rejection of contributions, temporary participation restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project-managed spaces.

Enforcement decisions should consider severity, context, repeated behavior, safety, security, research integrity, and the effect on the community. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by widely used open-source community conduct standards, including the Contributor Covenant, while being adapted for the engineering, security, and research-integrity needs of CyberPhotonics-SPR.
